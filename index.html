What We Do

Our Services

End-to-end bulk transport solutions tailored to the Ugandan construction and development sector.

01

Stone Haulage

We transport quarry stones, crushed aggregate, and boulders from quarries to your construction site. Our tipper trucks are purpose-built for rocky loads, ensuring safe and efficient delivery regardless of road conditions.

Quarry Stone Crushed Aggregate Boulders Hardcore
02

Sand Transport

River sand, lake sand, and washed sand delivered in bulk. We ensure correct moisture content and clean loads free of clay contaminants, critical for quality concrete and masonry work.

River Sand Lake Sand Washed Sand Fine Sand
03

Bulk Material Transport

Soil, murram, gravel, and general bulk cargo transport for road filling, land leveling, and site preparation. We handle large volumes under tight project timelines with our multi-truck dispatch system.

Soil & Murram Gravel Fill Material Road Base
04

Site Logistics & Scheduling

Full logistics management for large-scale projects. We coordinate fleet rotations, delivery schedules, and load tracking so your project never stalls due to material shortages. Dedicated site liaison officer included.

Fleet Management GPS Tracking Scheduled Delivery Reporting
